---
title: "Flux Statistics"
---

The flux statistics summarize the current map. Contributing Hits is the number of filtered surface interactions plotted into the map. Peak, minimum, average, standard deviation, and uniformity are computed from the displayed raster values.

With DNI scaling enabled, flux values are in W/m2 and plotted power is in W. The global centroid is the average world-space location of the plotted interactions; the local centroid reports the same point relative to the mapped element.
