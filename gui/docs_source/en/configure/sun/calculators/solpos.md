---
title: "SOLPOS 2.0 Sun Position"
---

SOLPOS 2.0 is an NLR-developed solar-position library written in C for engineering and renewable-energy applications. Compared with Duffie & Beckman, SOLPOS includes additional engineering corrections such as atmospheric refraction, equation-of-time handling, extraterrestrial-radiation calculations, and pressure-corrected air-mass models. This results in more complex calculations but more accurate sun-position predictions (approximately ±0.05°).

SOLPOS 2.0 is also used by NLR's SolarPILOT, allowing consistent solar-position workflows between the applications.

Reference: [NLR Solar Position and Intensity](https://www.nlr.gov/grid/solar-resource/solpos).
