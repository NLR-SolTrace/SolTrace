---
title: "Trace Options"
---

**Sun Shape** samples the configured angular emission profile. Enable it when you want realistic flux spread or final performance results. Disable it to use a single ideal incoming direction, which is useful for checking geometry, alignment, and nominal optical behavior.

**Optical Errors** applies the slope and specularity errors configured for each material. Enable it for tolerance studies and realistic performance estimates. Disable it to establish an ideal baseline or isolate geometry and alignment problems.

**Tracer Debug** is intended for runner-specific diagnostics when investigating a tracing problem, and should normally be left off. The current control is reserved for future diagnostic support and does not yet change trace behavior.
